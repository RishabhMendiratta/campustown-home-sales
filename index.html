How these numbers were computed

  • Price rule: sales under $10,000 are left out of every figure on this page (40 of 35,442 sales), because they are not plausible market prices. All medians are rounded to whole dollars.
  • Medians, not averages: the dataset contains a few sales over $5 million, up to $81.5 million, which would pull averages far above a typical sale.
  • Days on market is the time from list_date to contract_date, counted only for sales that have both dates. Sales with no listing on record are excluded rather than treated as zero.
  • Price per square foot skips homes whose living area is blank or recorded as 0.
  • Campus area is the dataset's university column: the nearest campus to each home, based on its coordinates.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
